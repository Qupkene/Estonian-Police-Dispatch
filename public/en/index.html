Police dispatch game on a real map of Estonia

EPD (Estonian Police Dispatch) is a free police dispatcher simulator that runs in the browser. Answer 112 emergency calls, decide how urgent each one is, send the right patrol and check people and vehicles in the database, across real Estonian towns and roads.

Play now, free

What you do

  • Answer 112 calls: ask where and what happened, spot hoaxes and set a priority from 1 to 4.
  • Dispatch patrols: pick the nearest suitable crew, call for back-up, the ambulance or the rescue service.
  • Run checks: search people and number plates, read licences, insurance and wanted notices, and answer the crews on the radio.
  • Manage a shift: fatigue, breaks, pursuits, arrests and a graded report at the end.

Over 400 kinds of incidents, four Estonian police prefectures, a career with sectors and story chapters, a daily challenge with a shared leaderboard, and multiplayer for up to four players (a race on the same shift, or one shared shift split by roles or sectors).

Language

The game is fully in English and Estonian; switch the language in the settings. The radio voice speaks Estonian.

Questions and answers

Is the game free?
Yes. EPD is completely free, with no ads, and nobody makes money from it.
Do I need to install anything?
No. It runs in the browser at politsei.eu, on a computer, tablet or phone.
Is this an official police game?
No. It is an unofficial fan game, not a product of the Estonian Police and Border Guard Board and not affiliated with it.
Can I play with friends?
Yes, up to four players per room, as a race or as one shared shift.
